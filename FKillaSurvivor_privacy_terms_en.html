Article 7 (Unity Ads and collection of the advertising ID)

The Game shows ads provided by Unity Technologies through Unity Ads. The ads are rewarded ads that you choose to watch. The Unity Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID and may use behavioral information such as your ad history for personalized ads.

  • What the advertising ID is: an identifier for advertising (Google advertising ID) assigned to each Android device, which you can delete or reset at any time.
  • Items collected: advertising ID, device information (model, OS version, language, etc.), IP address and approximate location, ad impression, view and click records, app usage time
  • How collected: automatically by the Unity Ads SDK when the Game runs and ads are shown (for this, the app declares the advertising ID permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)
  • Purposes: providing ads (including personalized ads), confirming that a rewarded ad was watched and giving the reward, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud
  • Retention: not stored by the Developer; kept by Unity for the period set out in Unity's privacy policy
  • How to opt out (device settings): on your Android device go to Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads and choose "Delete advertising ID" or turn off ad personalization. Menu names differ by device and Android version.
  • How to opt out (in the ad): tap the privacy icon (i) in a Unity ad to see the information Unity collected and opt out of personalized ads.
  • If you opt out you can still play the Game and watch rewarded ads; you will see general ads not based on your interests.

Article 8 (Automatic collection tools and how to refuse them)

The Game uses the advertising ID, which works like a web cookie, through Unity Ads. You can delete or reset it as described in Article 7. The identifier the Unity Ads SDK creates inside the app and your game data are deleted when you delete the app.
